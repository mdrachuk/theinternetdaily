Each chip carries the favicon of the place it points at. The glyph is inline SVG from tid.icons, so `read` items simply get nothing. #} {% macro byline(item, top=False) -%}

The byline goes first, above the title, and sits beside the title link rather than inside it: its chips are links of their own, and an anchor cannot hold another.
