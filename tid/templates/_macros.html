The chips come from the source type (tid.sources): a feed article has one, its source, pointing at the piece; a Hacker News story has two — the discussion, then the site it links to — and they are printed [Hacker News] → [github.com]. Each chip carries the favicon of the place it points at. The glyph is inline SVG from tid.icons, so `read` items simply get nothing. #} {% macro byline(item, top=False) -%}
